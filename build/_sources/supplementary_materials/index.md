# Supplementary materials

This section collects additional materials connected to publications, datasets, scripts, and methodological notes developed after _Corpus Approaches to Language in Social Media_.

The aim is to document technical extensions of the book’s core concerns, especially where later research outputs engage with the collection, processing, formatting, preservation, or analysis of digital textual data. Materials included here may therefore contain scripts, code examples, data samples, workflow notes, appendices, and other resources that support or expand published research.

Rather than functioning as a separate archive, this section should be read as a continuation of the compendium’s broader focus on **digital technicalities**: the technical, infrastructural, and procedural conditions that shape how digital data become usable corpus evidence.
